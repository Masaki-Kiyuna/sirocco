The neutral-H free-free binary CDF banks are little-endian private data
products with magic and version checks in source/hminus.c.  SIROCCO builds
the 8192-quantile H-minus radiative-attachment bank in memory from the CSV
cross section and adaptive temperature nodes.  It fails at startup on a wrong
magic, version, dimension, truncated read, or non-monotone table.

`Hminus_RA_milne_coefficient.csv` is generated by
`tools/generate_hminus_milne_table.c` from the McLaughlin single-detachment
cross section over 0.7542--10.95 eV.  It supplies the production spontaneous
RA coefficient on the same 368-node temperature grid as the CDF.  The
published attachment-rate table remains an independent comparison only.
